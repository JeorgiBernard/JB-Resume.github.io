How I run projects

My background in quality means I plan for risk from day one and don't call something done until it works.

01 · Plan

Scope the work

Turn requirements into a clear plan and tasks in Jira, with the biggest risks called out early.

02 · Coordinate

Run the team

Delegate work, track it to done, and keep multiple client projects moving in parallel.

03 · Communicate

Keep everyone aligned

Work directly with PMs and clients so issues are understood, documented and prioritized.

04 · Deliver

Ship it right

Check the work against what was promised before release, then close the loop with the client.

About

I came to tech as a mom of three, and I've been learning on purpose ever since.

When my kids started school, I joined Tech-Moms to try coding. I loved it, went on to SLCC's web development and UX/UI program, then Devmountain's QA automation program. Within a few years I went from student to coding coach, to trainer, to leading a team.

Tubie Journal comes from my own family's life with a feeding tube. Building it put me in the project owner's seat: I own the scope, the schedule and the release.

That's why I'm studying for the CAPM. I'm ready to move from checking projects to running them.
